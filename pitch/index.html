04 · How It Works

From dormant file to living network.

1

Dormancy

A creator uploads. Fans see only a 30-second clip, a blurred preview or the first three sentences.

2

Handshake

Two phones confirm proximity by NFC or BLE. Each person puts a fingerprint on the other's screen.

3

Bloom

They experience it in full, together. Then it unlocks for solo use and the bond is written on-chain.

4

Spread

The new SPORE shares with their own circle. Every hop is verified and traces to the Source.

Mass Bloom: at a concert, book launch or gallery opening, thousands unlock together, but only after each attendee completes a verified tap with another attendee.
